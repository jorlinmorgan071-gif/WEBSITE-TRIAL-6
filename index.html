The $1,299 laptop trap.

It looks like a flagship. It benchmarks like one. So why does it feel like a compromise by lunchtime?

For a week, I used this as my only computer: editing, writing, video calls, and one deeply unscientific number of browser tabs. The spec sheet tells half the story. The hinge, the heat, and that charger you have to bring everywhere tell the rest.

Here's what held up, what didn't, and who should actually spend the money.

THE GOOD: The screen is bright enough for a sunny desk, the keyboard has real travel, and the battery survived a full workday with 18% to spare.

THE CATCH: Under sustained load, the fans arrive early and the thin chassis gets warm exactly where your hands rest. At this price, the included 512GB storage feels like a deliberate upsell.

THE VERDICT: Great for everyday work. A frustrating buy for creators. If you need the extra power, save for the next tier; if you don't, the cheaper model is the smarter laptop.
