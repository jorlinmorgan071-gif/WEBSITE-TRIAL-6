RETURN POLICY

Coffee is final sale. Beans are roasted to order and shipped fresh, so opened (or opened-bag) coffee can't come back — but if a bag arrives damaged or the roast is off, email us within 14 days and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

Gear (Brew Kits & hardware)

Unopened brew gear in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery — through December 30, 2026 for Black Friday orders. You cover return shipping unless we shipped the wrong thing.

How to start a return

Email hello@northpaw.coffee with your order number and a photo of the item. We'll reply within 2 business days with a return label and instructions.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after the return reaches the roastery. Sale pricing is honored on exchanges for the same item.
